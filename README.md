# New Earth Promotional Website

## Context
New Earth is a fictional planet from a sci-fi world I came up with that takes place in the near future. NASA
has discovered a superhabitable planet in a solar system less than 50 lightyears from the Sol system. They 
call this planet "New Earth." It has everything our world has but is x1.2 the size, has a thicker atmosphere,
and much more habitable surface area. There's also signs of both plant life and animal life on this world,
giving it a potential source of food. NASA has developed a deconstructable colony ship that will be ready to
launch in 2043. NASA plans to get 700–800 colonists willing to make the generational trip to New Earth, which
is expected to take 400 years without assistance from future technology such as FTL craft catching up with the 
ship or engine enhancements made by the colonists mid-journey. 

This website is designed to promote NASA's mission to colonize this exoplanet and acquire colonists willing to
start the journey.

## Website Elements
- About page with countdown timer and tabs for different aspects of the mission (mission, purpose, planet, spacecraft, journey)
- Promo page to encourage visitors to sign up to be a colonist
- FAQ page
